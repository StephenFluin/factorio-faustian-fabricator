Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-28
  Features:
    - New graphics: the fabricator is now an occult altar. The soul orb, runes and glow only appear while it is powered.
    - Mining a leveled fabricator keeps its level, unspent upgrades and item pool on the item. Placing it again resumes the bargain.
    - Speed modules and beacons make items generate faster. Productivity gives a chance of extra items.
    - A heartbeat seals the deal when you spend an upgrade point.
    - GUI and event log text can now be translated, and items show their in-game names.
  Bugfixes:
    - The fabricator now draws its full configured power. Before, it only drew 1/30 of it.
    - Fixed the sprite being misaligned with the machine's footprint.
    - Fixed module slots missing on Factorio 2.0.
    - Fixed the event log being cut off after one line.
  Optimizations:
    - Less work per tick, especially with many fabricators.
  Compatibility:
    - Supports Factorio 2.0 (0.2.0) and 2.1 (0.2.1).
